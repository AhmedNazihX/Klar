The system is high-risk, and its high-risk rules apply from {date}. Also answer, under
"legacy", three key facts for Art. 111(2) (systems already on the market or in use before that
date), in the same form as the others:

- placed_before: the system was placed on the market or put into service before {date}, or will
  be. That date is still ahead, so a system the description shows already on the market or in
  use counts as "yes". A system still being built, or launched only on or after that date, is
  "no". When the description doesn't say, answer "unknown".
- significant_design_change: the system's design changes significantly on or after {date}, for
  example a new version that decides differently or serves another purpose; routine updates and
  bug fixes are not.
- for_public_authority: public authorities are meant to use the system, whether the company is
  one or supplies them.

When placed_before is "no", answer the other two "no" with evidence "assumed". Otherwise, when
the description doesn't settle one of them, answer it "unknown": never assume it away.
