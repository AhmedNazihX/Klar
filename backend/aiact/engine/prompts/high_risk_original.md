The text read here is the original AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as published in 2024), not
the text as amended by the Digital Omnibus. It has no Art. 6(1a), (1b) or (1c): where the
instructions name them, read `annex_i` by Art. 6(1) and the definition in Art. 3(14) alone.
- `safety_component_or_product`: "yes" when the AI system is itself the product, or is a component
  of it that fulfils a safety function for it, or whose failure or malfunctioning endangers the
  health and safety of persons or property (Art. 3(14)); otherwise "no".
- `third_party_assessment`: "yes" when that legislation requires the product to undergo a
  third-party conformity assessment before it is placed on the market or put into service,
  whatever risks the assessment covers (radio spectrum use and electromagnetic interference
  included); "no" when the manufacturer may assess conformity itself.
